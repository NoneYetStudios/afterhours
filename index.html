Controls

  • Desktop: WASD move · mouse aims · hold left-click to staple
  • Mobile: drag anywhere on the left side to move · hold STAPLE to fire (auto-aims at the nearest enemy, or drag the button to aim)
  • P / Esc pause · M sound · V voice

Survive

  • You keep the stapler. You do not keep the staples.
  • Grab glowing STAPLE REFILLS. They appear all over the floor, sometimes somewhere unwise, and they expire.
  • Score comes from survival time, eliminations and pickups.
  • There is no final level. Overtime is forever.
